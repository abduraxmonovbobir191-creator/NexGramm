# NexGramm — Privacy Policy / Maxfiylik siyosati

Effective date / Kuchga kirgan sana: 15 October 2026

NexGramm is an unofficial Telegram client (package `uz.nexgramm.app`). It is not made by or affiliated with Telegram.

## 1. Telegram data
Your chats, contacts, calls and media go between your phone and Telegram's servers through the Telegram API, as in
any Telegram app. Telegram's own privacy policy applies to them: https://telegram.org/privacy.
NexGramm does not copy your chats to its own servers.

## 2. NexGramm services
Some features use NexGramm's own server (`ng.nexecos.com`):

| Feature | What we receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in to NexGramm services (Lenta, Premium, NEX AI) | Telegram user id, first/last name, username | to create your NexGramm account |
| Lenta (feed) | the posts, photos, videos, stories, comments and likes you publish there, your profile name and photo | to show them to other Lenta users |
| NexGramm Premium (APK from our site only) | the payment receipt photo you send to @NexGrammBot | to confirm the payment by hand |
| Support / feedback | the text and screenshot you send to @NexGrammBot | to answer you |

## 3. NEX AI
NEX AI works only when you tap it and agree once. Only the text you choose (a question, a message, the recent
messages of one chat you open it in, or a short summary of an APK's declared permissions) is sent to the NEX AI
server to produce the answer. It is not used for advertising.

## 3a. Links as videos
When you send a message that is only an Instagram / TikTok / YouTube (and similar) link, NexGramm sends that link to
@SaveXStorageBot in your own Telegram account to get the video, then sends the video to your chat and removes the
helper messages from the bot chat. You can turn this off in NexGramm features → Comfort.

## 4. Ads
Users without Premium see ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and coarse data as
described in https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's
settings.

## 5. On your phone only
Chat locks, saved deleted / edited messages, theme and color settings, and the APK safety check are processed and
stored only on your phone. The APK check reads the APK's manifest on the phone; "Check on VirusTotal" opens the
VirusTotal site with the file's SHA-256 fingerprint only, never the file.

## 6. Sharing
We do not sell your data. We share it only with the services named above (Telegram, Google AdMob) or when the law
requires it.

## 7. Keeping and deleting your data
Lenta content stays until you delete it or your account. To delete your NexGramm account and all its data, write
"delete my account" to @NexGrammBot in Telegram; we delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app deletes the data
stored on the phone.

## 8. Children
NexGramm is not meant for children under 13 (16 in the EU).

## 9. Contact
Telegram: @NexGrammBot
